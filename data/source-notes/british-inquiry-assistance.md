Facts to extract from the cited report:
- Once Titanic's dangerous condition was understood, Captain Smith ordered messages sent to ships within wireless range.
- A CQD distress signal was transmitted at approximately 12:15 a.m.
- By approximately 12:25 a.m., Titanic had a corrected position and transmitted that it had struck an iceberg and required immediate assistance.
- Carpathia received the call and replied that she was coming.
- At approximately 12:26 a.m., Titanic transmitted that the ship was sinking and that escaping steam was interfering with wireless reception.
